About the Sales Tax Calculator

Sales tax, VAT and GST rates vary wildly between countries, states and even cities — from 0% to over 25%. This calculator handles any rate you type, and works in either direction: computing the tax-inclusive total for a quoted net price, or extracting the net price and tax amount from a tax-inclusive shelf price.

The reverse direction is the part most people get wrong. If a gross price already includes 20% tax, the net price is gross ÷ 1.20, not gross × 0.80. Multiplying by (1 − rate) is a very common mistake that overstates the tax — this tool uses the correct division so accountants and bookkeepers get exact figures.

Everything is computed locally in your browser with no data leaving your device, which matters when you are pricing confidential invoices or checking receipts. Results are rounded to two decimals for currency display, matching how real tax authorities round line items.

FAQ

What's the difference between this and the VAT calculator?

They use the same math; sales tax is typically charged at the point of sale in the US, while VAT/GST is charged throughout the supply chain in most other countries. This tool handles both.

Why is removing tax a division?

A gross price G includes tax at rate r, so G = net × (1 + r). Therefore net = G ÷ (1 + r). Simply multiplying G by (1 − r) gives the wrong net amount.

Can I use several decimal rates?

Yes — enter rates like 8.875% exactly; the calculator keeps full precision internally.
