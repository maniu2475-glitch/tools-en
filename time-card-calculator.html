About this tool

A time card calculator turns scattered clock-in and clock-out notes into a clean weekly total. Enter the times you started and finished each day, how many minutes of unpaid lunch you took, and the tool computes the exact paid hours — handling overnight shifts (a 22:00 to 06:00 shift correctly counts 8 hours, not negative 16) and lunch deductions automatically. If you fill in an hourly rate, it also shows your gross pay for the week, which is useful for checking that a paycheck matches the hours you actually worked. The default layout covers Monday to Friday; press Add row to include Saturday and Sunday for shift workers. Everything is rounded honestly: totals are exact to the minute, with the equivalent hours-and-minutes shown per day. The whole calculation runs in your browser, so your timesheet data stays private — nothing is uploaded to any server.
