About this Anagram Solver

An anagram solver takes a jumble of letters and reveals which valid words can be formed from some or all of them. It is the go-to helper for dozens of popular word games: unscrambling tiles in Scrabble and Words With Friends, cracking an anagram clue in a cryptic crossword, hunting for the best opening in Wordle, or simply satisfying curiosity when a friend poses a riddle. Traditionally you would need a thick dictionary and a lot of patience; this tool does the tedious part instantly and shows you the longest words first.

Enter your letters in the input box — spaces and punctuation are ignored, so you can paste a whole tile rack at once. The solver loops through its built-in dictionary of more than two thousand common English words, checks the letter counts for each candidate against your supply, and lists every word that can be spelled without reusing a letter more often than it appears. Toggle Exact length only if a puzzle requires you to use all your letters or a specific number of tiles. Results are sorted by length so the highest-scoring plays surface first, followed alphabetically.

The dictionary focuses on everyday English vocabulary rather than obscure two-letter Scrabble words, which keeps results relevant and readable. Because the word list and the matching logic are embedded directly in the page, the solver works instantly and never sends your letters to a server. It is fully private and works offline once loaded. Use it as a learning aid too: seeing which words your rack can form is a quick way to expand vocabulary and improve future gameplay. Bookmark it so it is always one tap away during a match.
