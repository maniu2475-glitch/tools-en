About this tool

The ISO 8601 standard defines weeks so that week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year, every week starts on Monday, and the 52nd or 53rd week ends the year. This creates two quirks people trip over constantly: December 29-31 can already belong to week 1 of the next year, and January 1-3 can still belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous year. That is why US-style numbering (week 1 = the week containing January 1) and ISO numbering disagree in many years. This calculator works both directions: enter a date and it shows the ISO week number, the ISO year-week label, and the exact Monday-to-Sunday range of that week; or enter a year and week number and it resolves the dates. It uses the well-known Thursday-based algorithm and a 53-week check (a year has 53 ISO weeks when January 1 is a Thursday, or a leap year starting on Wednesday). Everything runs locally in JavaScript.
