About this tool

This random number generator produces random integers or decimals between any two bounds you choose. Enable the unique option to prevent repeats — perfect for lottery picks, raffle draws, sampling, gaming (d6, d20 dice) or classroom activities. It uses the browser's cryptographic random source (crypto.getRandomValues) rather than plain Math.random, so the output is strong enough for shuffles, giveaways and unbiased sampling. You can generate a single number or a batch of many at once, with negative ranges and decimals supported. Everything runs locally in your browser: no data is uploaded and no account is required.
