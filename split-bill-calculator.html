About this tool

Splitting a shared bill is simple arithmetic that somehow always goes wrong at the table: someone forgets the tip, the rounding leaves the total short, and the last person ends up paying the odd cents. This calculator does the whole job in one step: enter the pre-tip bill, the number of people, and any tip or tax percentage, and every figure is computed for you.

The tool uses ceiling rounding to the nearest cent for each share, so when a total does not divide evenly the shares always add up to at least the bill — never leaving the group short. The output shows the tip amount, the grand total, each person's share, and the total collected.

It works with any currency, because you simply type the numbers as they appear on the receipt. For restaurant-specific tipping presets and per-person results, also see our tip calculator; for tracking who owes whom over time, a simple note-taking pass through our online notepad works well. Everything runs locally in your browser.

FAQ

What if the bill doesn't divide evenly?

Each share is rounded up to the nearest cent, so the shares always cover the full bill — someone may pay one cent more, but nobody underpays.

How do I handle an uneven split?

Add up each person's items separately, then run each subtotal through the calculator with the same tip percentage to keep it fair.

Does it work for any currency?

Yes. The tool only does arithmetic on the numbers you type, so use dollars, euros, yuan or anything else.
