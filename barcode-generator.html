Code 128 barcode

Code 128 is one of the most widely used barcode symbologies in logistics, shipping and inventory management. Unlike simple numeric formats such as EAN/UPC, Code 128 can encode all 128 ASCII characters, making it suitable for serial numbers, order IDs, tracking codes and any alphanumeric label. Its compact high-density design and built-in checksum digit make scanners read it reliably even at small sizes, which is why warehouses, couriers and point-of-sale systems rely on it worldwide.

Enter any text and this generator draws a print-ready Code 128 barcode instantly - no accounts, no watermarks, no limits. Adjust the bar height and scale to match your printer, then download the barcode as a PNG image at 2x resolution or as a scalable SVG file for print shops. The barcode is rendered entirely by JavaScript in your browser, so the data you encode (which may include confidential order or customer IDs) is never transmitted anywhere. It is perfect for one-off shipping labels, asset tags, event tickets or classroom projects.
