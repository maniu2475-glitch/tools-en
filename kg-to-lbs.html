About this tool

Kilograms are the standard unit of mass almost everywhere in the world, while pounds (lb) and ounces (oz) are still used for body weight, gym plates and produce in the United States, Liberia and a few other places. The exact conversion factor is 1 kilogram = 2.2046226218 pounds, and one pound contains 16 ounces. Because the factor is irrational-looking, mental math gets messy fast — 70 kg is about 154.3 lb, and 200 lb is roughly 90.7 kg. This converter does the arithmetic instantly in both directions and also breaks pounds into the familiar pound + ounce format used on scales. It shows nearby values in a small table so you can sanity-check your gym progress or a recipe without re-typing. The tool is pure client-side JavaScript: your data never leaves the browser.
