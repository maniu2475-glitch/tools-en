Remove line breaks from any text online: strip all newlines, or replace them with spaces or custom separators.

About the Remove Line Breaks

This tool removes line breaks from any text in one click. Paste text copied from a PDF, an email, a spreadsheet or a web page and choose what to do with the line breaks: remove them entirely (merging everything into one continuous line), replace each break with a single space to keep words readable, or join paragraphs with a custom separator such as a comma. An extra option removes fully empty lines only, which is handy for cleaning exported lists where every row has a trailing newline. Common uses include fixing PDF text that is broken into short lines, preparing addresses or descriptions for single-line form fields, cleaning keyword lists, and formatting data before pasting into Excel. All processing happens locally in your browser, so confidential text never leaves your device.
