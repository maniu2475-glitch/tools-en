About this tool

Simple interest is the easiest way to price the cost of borrowing money or the return on a deposit: interest equals principal times rate times time, or I = P × r × t. Unlike compound interest, simple interest is charged only on the original principal, so a $1,000 deposit at 5% per year earns exactly $50 each year — $150 over three years. This calculator returns the total interest and the maturity amount (P + I) for any combination of inputs, and you can also rearrange it mentally to solve for a missing value: for example, if you know the interest paid and the term, divide interest by (P × t) to recover the implied annual rate. Simple interest is commonly used for short-term personal loans, auto-title lending, some bonds and Treasury bills, and late-payment fees. All arithmetic runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded, so sensitive loan figures stay on your machine.
