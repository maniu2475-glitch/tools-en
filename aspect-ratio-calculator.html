About this tool

Whenever you resize an image, thumbnail, or video, keeping the aspect ratio is what prevents stretched or squashed results. This calculator does the math for you: enter the original dimensions, then type either a target width or a target height, and the matching dimension appears instantly. It also reduces the ratio to its simplest form (1920x1080 becomes 16:9) so you can compare against standard formats at a glance. Designers use it to check whether footage fits a frame, developers to compute responsive image sizes, and video editors to letterbox correctly for social platforms. Quick buttons for the most common ratios - 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 21:9 and 9:16 vertical - make one-off conversions a single click.
