About this tool

Paste any list of numbers and instantly get the mean, median, mode, sum, count, minimum, maximum, range and population standard deviation. The parser accepts commas, spaces, semicolons or new-line separated values, so you can copy data straight out of a spreadsheet, survey export or homework problem without cleaning it first. The mean is the ordinary arithmetic average — the sum divided by how many values there are — while the median is the middle value once the list is sorted, which resists distortion by outliers. The mode is the most frequent value, reported only when something repeats. Standard deviation measures how spread out the numbers are around the mean, useful in statistics coursework, quality checks and sports analytics. All math happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded, making it safe for confidential figures.
