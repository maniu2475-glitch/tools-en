About this tool

Decimals and fractions are two ways of writing the same numbers, and switching between them is constant homework in school math and a real need in carpentry, machining, cooking and finance: 0.375 inch is exactly 3/8", and drill charts are written in fractions. Converting a decimal to a fraction means multiplying by a power of ten and reducing by the greatest common divisor - this calculator does it instantly and always shows the fully simplified result, plus converts fractions back to decimals with full precision. Repeat decimals like 0.333... are shown rounded to 10 decimal places. Everything is computed locally in your browser.
