About this tool

Large images slow down websites, clog up email inboxes and eat mobile data. This compressor re-encodes your picture on the device using the browser's built-in canvas encoder, letting you trade a small amount of visual quality for a much smaller file. Drag in a product photo, a screenshot or a photo from your phone, move the quality slider until the preview still looks sharp, and download the result.

The tool supports any image format your browser can decode - JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame) and BMP. PNG files keep their lossless format, so the biggest savings come from the optional max width resize, which is also the single most effective way to shrink screenshots for documentation or bug reports. JPEG and WebP inputs are re-encoded at your chosen quality, and a quality of 0.75 to 0.85 is usually indistinguishable from the original while cutting the file size by half or more.

Because nothing is uploaded, the tool works offline once the page has loaded, is safe for confidential screenshots and contracts, and has no file size limit other than your own device memory. No signup, no watermark, no daily quota. If you need to convert between formats instead, try the PNG to JPG converter; if you need a data URI for CSS, use the Image to Base64 tool.
