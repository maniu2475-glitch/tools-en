About this tool

A simple, fast coin flip simulator for settling decisions, picking who goes first, or teaching basic probability. Each flip uses a fresh random number from your browser, giving a fair 50/50 chance between heads and tails — no server involved, so it works even offline once the page is loaded. The animated toss spins the coin briefly before landing, which makes classroom demonstrations more fun and prevents the "flip again until I like it" argument. A statistics counter below tracks how many heads, tails and total flips you have made, so you can watch the long-run ratio converge toward 50/50, a classic experiment in introductory probability lessons. Use it for games, sports kick-offs, binary choices, or anywhere you need an unbiased instant decision.
