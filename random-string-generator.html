Random String Generator

Cryptographically secure random strings — generated locally in your browser, never uploaded.

Set your options and press Generate.

About this tool

This random string generator uses your browser's built-in cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), not Math.random, so the output is suitable for passwords, API keys, session tokens, coupon codes, salts and test fixtures. Pick a length, choose the character sets you need, optionally strip look-alike characters such as 0/O and 1/l/I to make strings easier to read out loud, and generate up to a hundred strings at once. Everything runs locally on your device: no string, length or option is ever sent to a server, which means you can safely use it for real credentials. For best security, use at least 16 characters with upper and lower case, digits and symbols.
