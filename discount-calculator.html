About this tool

This discount calculator solves both directions of every sale. Forward: you know the original price and the advertised discount percentage — it computes the final price and exactly how much you save. Reverse: you see a price tag and a was-price — it tells you the actual percentage discount the store is offering, so a 'huge sale' can be checked in seconds. It handles stacked discounts (an extra 20% off an already discounted price), coupon amounts, tax-inclusive comparisons and decimals. Useful during Black Friday, seasonal sales or when comparing deals between shops. Everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and results update the moment you type.
