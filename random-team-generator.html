Random Team Generator

Split any list of names into fair random teams — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

About this tool

Whether you are organizing a PE class, an office icebreaker, a LAN party or a hackathon, dividing people into fair teams by hand is slow and looks biased. This random team generator takes one name per line, shuffles the list with the Fisher–Yates algorithm (an unbiased shuffle used in real lotteries and card games), and then deals the names out round-robin into the number of teams you request, so team sizes differ by at most one person. The output is plain text that you can copy straight into a chat window or projector slide. Each click reshuffles, so for a best-of-three draft you can regenerate quickly. Unlike draws from a hat, the shuffle is instant for hundreds of participants and no name can be lost on the floor. Everything happens locally in your browser — the list of names is never uploaded anywhere, which matters when the roster contains customer or student data.
