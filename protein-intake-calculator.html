Protein Intake Calculator

Your daily protein target in grams, by goal and activity - everything runs in your browser.

About this tool

Protein is the macronutrient your body uses to build and repair muscle, skin, hormones and enzymes, and hitting the right daily amount matters whether your goal is performance or simple healthy ageing. Research-backed guidelines range from about 0.8 g per kg of body weight for sedentary adults (the minimum RDA), up to 1.6-2.2 g/kg for people building muscle, and similar high intakes are useful during fat loss to protect lean mass. This calculator takes your body weight, units, goal and training frequency and applies those evidence-based multipliers to give a daily gram target plus a per-meal hint assuming four protein-containing meals. It also shows roughly how that translates into common foods - for example one medium chicken breast or a cup of cooked lentils - so the number becomes actionable. Everything is computed locally in JavaScript; no health data is uploaded anywhere.
