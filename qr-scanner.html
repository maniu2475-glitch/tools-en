About this tool

A QR code scanner that works entirely offline in your browser. Click "Start camera" to point your webcam at a QR code - the page detects and decodes it automatically using the built-in BarcodeDetector API. Alternatively, upload a screenshot or photo that contains a QR code and the tool reads it instantly. Because everything runs locally, confidential codes such as payment or 2FA QR images are never sent to any server. Works great for decoding Wi-Fi passwords, event tickets, links on posters, and TOTP secrets for authenticator apps. For creating codes instead, use the related QR code generator tool.
