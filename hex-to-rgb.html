About this tool

Hexadecimal color codes are the standard way to describe colors on the web: #FF6600 means 255 parts red, 102 parts green and 0 parts blue, each channel ranging from 0 to 255. This converter works in both directions — paste a hex code to get the RGB triplet, or enter red, green and blue values to get the hex string back — and it also shows the CSS rgb() notation and the HSL equivalent, which is often easier when you want to lighten or darken a color while keeping its hue. Typing #FFF or even FFF works too, since three-digit shorthand expands automatically. Everything is computed locally in your browser, so you can paste any brand color without it ever being sent anywhere.
