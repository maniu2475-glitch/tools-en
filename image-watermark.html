About this tool

A watermark is the simplest way to mark an image as yours before it gets shared, re-posted or scraped. Photographers use a copyright line, designers use a brand name, and marketplace sellers use it to protect product photos from being reused by competitors. This tool draws a text watermark straight onto a canvas in your browser: choose the text, font size, opacity, color and position, then export a PNG. Position presets cover the four corners plus a centered placement, and the tiled option repeats the watermark across the whole image, which is much harder to crop out than a single corner mark. A drop shadow is applied by default because white text over a bright sky or a white background becomes invisible without it. Because the image is decoded by the browser and never transmitted, this is safe for client photos, ID documents and unreleased product shots — nothing leaves your computer and no copy is stored. Practical tips: keep opacity around 30-50% so the watermark deters reuse without ruining the picture, keep the font size proportional to image width (about 4-6% looks natural), and place the mark where cropping it out would cut the subject. Export at the original resolution using the download button; the PNG keeps full quality. If you need to shrink the file afterwards, run it through our image compressor.
