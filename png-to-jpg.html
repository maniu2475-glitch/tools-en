About this tool

PNG is a lossless format that is ideal for screenshots, icons and graphics with sharp edges, but photographic images stored as PNG can easily be ten times larger than the equivalent JPEG. Converting to JPG lets you control the quality/size trade-off: at 90-95% quality most photos are visually indistinguishable from the source while occupying a fraction of the disk space, which is exactly what you need before uploading to a CMS, sending by email or embedding in a slide deck.

Because JPEG has no alpha channel, every transparent pixel must be flattened onto a solid colour. This converter draws your PNG onto a canvas filled with the colour you pick (white by default) before encoding, so logos with transparency come out clean instead of showing black background artifacts. Pick a colour that matches the destination page background for the best result.

Everything happens locally through the Canvas API and canvas.toBlob('image/jpeg', quality) - your images are never uploaded to any server, which matters if you are converting ID scans, medical images, unreleased product shots or client work under NDA. There are no file-size limits other than your device's memory, and no daily quota.

Tips for the smallest acceptable file: lower quality to around 80% for web thumbnails, resize large photos before converting (a 4000px photo displayed at 800px wastes bandwidth), and remember that repeated re-encoding degrades quality, so always convert from the original PNG rather than from an already compressed JPG. Need the opposite direction? Try the image to Base64 encoder.
