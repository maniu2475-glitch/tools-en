Text to Speech (TTS) Reader

About This Tool

Free online text to speech reader that converts your text into natural speech using your browser's built-in voices. No upload, no signup, works instantly.

This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript, which means your data never leaves your device. There is no signup, no upload and no server-side processing. It works offline once the page is loaded, loads instantly on both desktop and mobile, and is completely free to use. Developers, students, designers and everyday users rely on this kind of utility because it removes the friction of installing software just to solve one small problem. If you use this tool regularly, consider bookmarking it for quick access.
