About the Color Contrast Checker

Low-contrast text is the single most common accessibility failure on the web, and WCAG 2.1 sets exact numeric thresholds: normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for level AA and 7:1 for level AAA, while large text (18pt, or 14pt bold, and above) needs 3:1 for AA and 4.5:1 for AAA. Icons and form borders need 3:1 under the non-text contrast rule 1.4.11. This tool computes the ratio from two hex colours using the official relative-luminance formula - it linearises each sRGB channel, weights them by 0.2126, 0.7152 and 0.0722, then applies (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) - and shows pass/fail badges for every threshold at once. A live preview renders sample text in your chosen colours so you can judge readability, and the 'suggest accessible colour' button finds the better of black or white and applies it instantly. Designers use it while building design systems and brand palettes; developers use it before shipping a marketing page or a dark theme. All computation is local.
