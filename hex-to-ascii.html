Hex ⇄ ASCII Text Converter

About this tool

Every character in a computer is stored as a number, and hexadecimal is the most common way humans write those numbers down. Hex-to-ASCII conversion is a daily task for anyone working with network packets, debugging file formats, reverse engineering, or reading memory dumps: 0x48 0x65 0x6c 0x6c 0x6f spells "Hello". This converter accepts hex with or without spaces, colons or 0x prefixes, shows a dot for non-printable bytes, and also converts in the reverse direction so you can check what your text looks like on the wire. Everything runs locally in your browser.
