About this tool

A fuel cost calculator answers three everyday questions: how much will a trip cost, how far can I drive on a tank, and how much do I spend per month commuting. You enter the distance, your vehicle's fuel efficiency — miles per gallon or liters per 100 kilometers, the conversion is handled automatically — and the fuel price, and the tool returns the total cost plus a per-mile or per-kilometer figure that is useful for comparing cars, reimbursing travel expenses or pricing delivery routes. A typical gasoline car in the United States averages about 25 mpg, while a diesel sedan in Europe might use 5 L/100 km. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so no trip details ever leave your device.
