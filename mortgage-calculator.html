Mortgage Calculator

Monthly payment, total interest and payoff summary for any fixed-rate home loan - everything runs in your browser.

About this tool

A mortgage is typically the largest debt a household ever takes on, so knowing the true monthly cost before you sign matters. This fixed-rate mortgage calculator uses the standard amortization formula: with principal P, monthly interest rate r (annual rate divided by 12) and n total monthly payments, the payment equals P x r / (1 - (1+r)^-n). Enter the home price, your down payment, the annual interest rate and the loan term, and you instantly get the monthly payment broken into the loan-amount portion and an optional rough estimate of property tax, insurance and PMI, plus the total interest you would pay over the life of the loan. Comparing a few scenarios - a shorter 15-year term versus 30 years, or a larger down payment - often reveals five-figure savings, and doing that comparison here costs nothing. Every calculation happens locally in JavaScript; your financial data never leaves your device.
