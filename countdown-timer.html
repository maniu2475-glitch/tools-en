About this tool

A free online countdown timer with hours, minutes and seconds display, plus one-click presets including the popular 25-minute Pomodoro interval. Start, pause and reset controls make it suitable for workout circuits, exam practice, cooking, meetings and study sessions. The large tabular digits stay readable from across a room, and the display highlights in the site's accent colour while counting down. Everything runs locally in your browser: keep the tab open and the timer keeps ticking even if your internet drops, since no server round-trips are involved. When time runs out the status line switches to a clear "Time is up!" message you can see at a glance. There is nothing to install and no account to create — just set your duration and press Start.
