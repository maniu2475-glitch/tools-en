About the Online Calculator

A free online calculator for everyday arithmetic: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages and square roots. It works just like a physical calculator - you can click the on-screen buttons or use your keyboard number pad, operators, Enter for equals, Escape to clear, and Backspace to delete a digit. A small display above the main one shows the running expression so you always know what you typed. The memory keys (M+, M-, MR, MC) let you store intermediate results while doing multi-step math such as splitting a bill, checking an invoice, or converting units by hand. Everything runs locally in your browser with no installation, no account and no data upload, so you can safely use it for any numbers, including private or financial values.
