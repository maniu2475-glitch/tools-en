Generate printable times tables from 1 to 20 — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

About this tool

The multiplication table — often credited to Pythagoras and still called the Table of Pythagoras in many countries — is the backbone of elementary arithmetic. Memorizing products up to 10 or 12 turns later skills like long division, fractions, factoring and mental percentage math from slow, error-prone calculations into instant recall. This generator renders a clean grid from 1×1 up to 20×20: choose the size, and the row and column you are currently studying get highlighted so the pattern of that table stands out visually. The grid prints nicely on one page (use your browser's print dialog; it is pure HTML, so the print layout stays crisp). A built-in quiz mode fires ten random questions drawn from the selected range — great for two-minute daily drills; the answer is checked instantly and your streak is shown. Because the page is fully static and offline-capable, it works on any school laptop or tablet with no install and no tracking. For more arithmetic practice try the binary calculator, the fraction calculator and the LCM/GCD calculator linked below.
