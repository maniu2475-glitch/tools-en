About this tool

Markup and margin describe the same profit from two different angles, and confusing them is one of the most common mistakes in small-business pricing. Markup is profit expressed as a percentage of cost: buy for $80 and sell for $100 and the markup is 25%. Margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price — in that same example, $20 on $100, so the margin is only 20%. That means a 25% markup is a 20% margin, a 100% markup is a 50% margin, and so on. This calculator accepts any two of cost, selling price, markup or margin and fills in the rest instantly, so you can move between wholesale cost sheets, retail price tags and financial statements without doing the algebra by hand. Everything is computed in your browser.
