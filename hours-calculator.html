Hours Calculator

Compute hours between two clock times — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

About this tool

Adding up working hours sounds trivial until you meet a night shift that starts at 21:00 and ends at 05:00, or a timesheet that wants decimals rather than hours and minutes. This hours calculator handles all three problems at once. It parses both clock times, detects when the end time is earlier than the start (meaning the shift crossed midnight) and rolls the day over automatically. An optional unpaid break in minutes is subtracted from the gross duration, which is how most payroll systems compute payable time. The result is shown in two formats: human-readable "h:mm" for schedules and decimal hours ("8.50") for spreadsheets, plus an estimated pay amount when you enter an hourly rate. Everything is computed locally in JavaScript, so you can type real payroll data without any privacy worry. Combine it with the time card calculator for weekly totals or the date difference calculator for multi-day spans.
