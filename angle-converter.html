About this tool

Angles appear in trigonometry homework, CAD drawings, astronomy coordinates, satellite pointing, game development and machining. The four main systems each divide a full circle differently: 360 degrees, 2π radians, 400 gradians, or 1 turn. Radians are the natural unit of mathematics because sin, cos and tan in calculus assume them; degrees dominate everyday navigation and engineering drawings; gradians (also called gons) survive in European surveying where a right angle is exactly 100 gon; and the mil (milliradian) is used for artillery and rifle scopes, where 1 mil ≈ 1 metre at 1000 metres. This converter also handles arcminutes and arcseconds, the 1/60 and 1/3600 subdivisions used in astronomy and GPS coordinates. Conversions use the exact factor 180/π per radian. Enter any value including decimals or numbers beyond a full turn, and the result is computed instantly in your browser - no login, no limits, nothing sent to a server.
