About this tool

This speed converter instantly translates between the six most common velocity units: kilometres per hour (km/h), miles per hour (mph), metres per second (m/s), knots (kn), feet per second (ft/s) and centimetres per second. Each field uses exact conversion factors, so 100 km/h becomes 62.137119 mph and 60 mph becomes exactly 96.56064 km/h. The tool is handy for drivers comparing speed limits between countries, runners checking treadmill paces, sailors and pilots working with knots, physics students converting to SI units, and engineers reading fan or airflow specifications. Type any number, pick the two units, and the answer appears immediately - there is no server round trip, because all math runs locally in your browser. That makes it safe, fast and available offline once loaded. Common reference points: highway speeds are around 100-130 km/h, a marathon runner averages roughly 12 km/h, and the speed of sound is about 1,235 km/h or 767 mph.
