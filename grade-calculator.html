About this tool

Students constantly ask two questions: "What is my grade right now?" and "What do I need on the final to reach the grade I want?" This calculator answers both. Graded components in most courses are weighted — homework 20%, quizzes 30%, midterm 25%, final 25% — so simply averaging raw scores gives the wrong answer. The calculator computes the weighted average: each score is multiplied by its weight, the products are summed, and the total is divided by the sum of entered weights, which also lets you enter partial data (e.g. only the work graded so far). The second part solves the final-exam equation algebraically: if your current weighted average is C, the final is worth W% and you want target T, then the required final score is (T − C·(100−W)/100)·100/W. If the required score is above 100% it will say so honestly, meaning the target is mathematically out of reach and extra credit is the only option; below 0% means you have already secured the target. All math runs locally in your browser — perfect for checking grades on a phone during class without installing anything.
