Reading Time Calculator

About this tool

Reading time calculators are used by bloggers, content marketers and web editors to show visitors how long an article will take to read — the little "6 min read" badge next to a headline. The standard average adult reading speed is about 200 to 250 words per minute for English prose; this tool uses 238 wpm by default but lets you change it to 150 for difficult technical material or 300 for skimming. It also counts words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, and estimates speaking time at a typical presentation pace of 140 words per minute, which is handy when preparing a talk or video script. All counting happens locally in your browser, so you can paste unpublished drafts safely.
