About this tool

This calculator answers the classic question "how long will this download take?" by combining file size, connection speed and real-world efficiency. The key insight it encodes is the difference between megabits (Mb) and megabytes (MB): internet plans are sold in Mbps but files are measured in MB, and 8 megabits equal 1 megabyte, so a 100 Mbps line moves at most 12.5 MB/s. On top of that, protocol overhead, Wi-Fi loss, disk speed and server limits typically reduce throughput to around 80-95 percent of the theoretical rate - adjustable with the efficiency field. Enter a 70 GB game on a 300 Mbps fibre line and you will see roughly 35 minutes; a 4 GB movie on 25 Mbps ADSL needs about 25 minutes. The tool supports file sizes in KB/MB/GB and speeds in Mbps, MB/s, Gbps or KB/s, then formats the result in seconds, minutes or hours automatically. All calculations run locally in your browser, so it works offline and your inputs are never transmitted anywhere.
