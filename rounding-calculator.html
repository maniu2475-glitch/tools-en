Rounding Calculator

Enter a number to round.

About this tool

Rounding numbers is one of the most common everyday math tasks, from splitting a bill to reporting measurements. This rounding calculator shows three results at once: normal rounding (half up) to the number of decimal places you choose, rounding up to the next step, and rounding down. The integer row shows the nearest whole number. You can use negative decimal places to round to tens, hundreds or thousands. For example, rounding 1234.5678 to −2 places gives 1200. Results appear instantly, everything is computed locally in your browser, and there are no limits on how many numbers you can round.
