About this tool

This loan calculator answers the three questions every borrower asks: what is my monthly payment, how much interest will I pay in total, and how much will the loan really cost me overall. Enter the loan amount (principal), the annual interest rate and the number of years, and the standard amortization formula is applied instantly. The monthly payment is computed as P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of monthly payments. Total interest is simply the sum of all payments minus the principal. Useful for mortgages, auto loans, student loans and personal loans — compare scenarios side by side to see how a shorter term or a lower rate changes the total cost. Everything runs locally in your browser with no data uploaded, so your financial figures stay private.
