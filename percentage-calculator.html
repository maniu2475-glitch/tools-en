About this tool

Percentages appear everywhere - discounts, taxes, tips, interest rates, exam scores and growth reports - yet mental arithmetic with them is surprisingly error-prone. This calculator covers the three percentage questions people ask most: "what is X percent of Y", "X is what percent of Y", and "what is the percentage change from X to Y". Type any two values and the third is computed instantly, with the formula shown so you can verify the result by hand. Everything runs locally in your browser: no data is uploaded, no account is needed, and results appear the moment you type. Bookmark it for shopping season, homework, or financial planning; it also handles decimals and negative numbers, so percentage decreases and losses are supported too.
