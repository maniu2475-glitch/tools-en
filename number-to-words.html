Number to Words Converter

Number → English words

Writing numbers out in words is a common requirement when filling out bank checks, legal documents, invoices, and educational exercises. Rules differ slightly between American and British English, and beyond a few thousand it is easy to make mistakes with millions, billions and trillions. This converter spells out any positive integer up to 15 digits instantly and accurately, using standard American English conventions - for example 1234 becomes "one thousand two hundred thirty-four", and 2026 becomes "two thousand twenty-six".

Just type a number and the words appear immediately; you can copy the result with one click. The tool also adds commas every three digits to the numeric display so long numbers are easy to read, and can capitalize the result for check-writing use. Everything is computed in your browser with plain JavaScript - no servers, no tracking - so it works offline and handles as many conversions as you like. Bookmark it for accounting, teaching kids numbers, or any time you need to spell a number correctly.
