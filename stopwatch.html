About the Online Stopwatch

This stopwatch uses the browser's high-resolution performance timer rather than counting timer ticks, so it stays accurate even if the tab is briefly throttled — the displayed time is always computed from real elapsed wall-clock time with 10-millisecond resolution shown.

Lap times are the heart of any serious stopwatch: each press of Lap records a split without stopping the clock, and the tool lists both the individual lap time and the cumulative elapsed time. This is ideal for training intervals, cooking stages, lab experiments or timing any repeated activity.

Unlike native apps, this stopwatch requires no install and no permissions. It works on desktop and mobile, keeps running while you switch windows, and stores nothing on any server — your timing data stays entirely on your device.
