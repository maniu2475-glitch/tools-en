About this tool

Value Added Tax (VAT) — called GST in some countries — is added at every stage of production and paid by the final consumer. Businesses constantly need to move between net and gross prices: invoicing a client, checking a supplier's bill, or filing a VAT return. The math is simple but easy to get wrong when tired: adding VAT is price × (1 + rate/100), while removing it from a gross figure is price ÷ (1 + rate/100), not price minus rate%.

This calculator does both directions correctly. Quick buttons cover the most common rates: 20% (UK standard), 19% (Germany), 10% (many reduced rates) and 5% (UK reduced). You can also type any custom rate for countries like Denmark's 25% or Hungary's 27%.

The result box shows the net price, VAT amount and gross total so you can copy all three figures straight into an invoice. All calculations run locally, making it safe for confidential pricing data. It works with any currency, so it is equally useful for USD, EUR, GBP or any other books.

FAQ

Is removing VAT just subtracting the percentage?

No. To remove 20% VAT from 120 you divide by 1.2 to get 100, not subtract 20% (which would give 96). The tool always uses the correct method.

Which VAT rate should I use?

It depends on the country and product category. The buttons cover common rates; always confirm the applicable rate for your jurisdiction.

Can I use it for GST or sales tax?

Yes. The arithmetic is identical for any percentage-based consumption tax.
