About this tool

The greatest common divisor (GCD, also called the highest common factor HCF) of two integers is the largest number dividing both, and the least common multiple (LCM) is the smallest number both divide into. They are workhorses of school arithmetic: simplifying the fraction 42/56 means dividing by the GCD 14, and scheduling two buses that leave every 12 and 18 minutes means finding the LCM 36. This calculator accepts any comma-separated list of integers, computes both values, and shows the classic Euclidean algorithm steps — repeated division with remainder — so you can see how the answer was reached instead of trusting a black box. Results update instantly as you edit the list, and negative numbers are handled by using their absolute values, since divisors are defined on magnitude.
