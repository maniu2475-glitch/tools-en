About this tool

Cooking recipes, chemistry labs, aquariums and fuel economy all measure volume, but they use wildly different units. This converter covers metric units (millilitres, litres, cubic metres), US customary units (teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons), imperial units (imperial fluid ounces, pints, gallons) and cubic feet / cubic inches. All factors are exact legal definitions: 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 litres, 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 litres, 1 US cup = 236.5882365 ml. Note that US and imperial cups, pints and gallons differ - a US gallon is about 0.83 imperial gallons - which is why British and American recipes never quite match. Use this tool to scale a recipe, convert a car's fuel tank size, check a drink label, or convert lab quantities. Everything is calculated in your browser with no signup, no ads inside the tool, and your input never leaves your device.
