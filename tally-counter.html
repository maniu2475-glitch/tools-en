About this tool

A tally counter (also called a click counter or clicker) is one of the simplest and most useful counting tools. People use it to count people entering an event, tally scores in a game, count laps, reps at the gym, inventory items, or tally survey responses. This online version replaces the physical mechanical clicker: just click the big button or tap the space bar. You can set a custom step (useful when counting in dozens or boxes of 12), undo mistakes, and the value is stored in your browser's localStorage so an accidental page refresh won't wipe your count. Like every tool on this site it runs 100% client-side and works offline once loaded.
