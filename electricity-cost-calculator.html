Electricity Cost Calculator

Daily, monthly and yearly running cost for any appliance - everything runs in your browser.

About this tool

Have you ever wondered what that old space heater, gaming PC or spare freezer actually costs you? The math is simple but tedious: an appliance's power in watts times the hours it runs per day gives watt-hours; divide by 1000 to get kilowatt-hours, then multiply by the price your utility charges per kWh. This calculator does it instantly and extends the result to a monthly (30-day) and yearly figure, so a 1500 W heater used 3 hours a day at $0.15/kWh turns out to cost about $20 a month - exactly the kind of number that changes behaviour. You can also use it to compare appliances before buying, or to sanity-check an estimated bill. Typical rates range from roughly $0.08 to $0.35 per kWh depending on country and plan; check your latest utility bill for the exact figure. All computation is local JavaScript - nothing is sent anywhere.
