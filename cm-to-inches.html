About this tool

Centimeters and inches are the two most widely used units for height, screen sizes and small measurements. This converter uses the exact definition 1 inch = 2.54 cm, adopted internationally in 1959. Type in either box and the other updates instantly; the result line also shows a feet-and-inches breakdown, which is the usual way heights are quoted in the United States. The quick reference table covers common heights from 150 cm to 190 cm. Common conversions: 30 cm ≈ 11.81 in (a ruler), 100 cm = 39.37 in, 168 cm ≈ 5 ft 6 in. The tool is free, unlimited and runs completely in your browser.
