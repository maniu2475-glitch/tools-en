Word ⇄ Number Converter

About this tool

Writing numbers as English words ("seventy-two thousand five hundred") appears on legal documents, cheques, invoices, song lyrics, subtitles, and NLP training data. Converting them back to digits is a classic interview question and a real-world chore: you must handle hyphens, "and", compound scales like "two hundred fifty thousand", and the short-scale words up to a trillion. This converter parses all of that and also works in reverse, turning 1,250,000 into "one million two hundred fifty thousand" using British-style "and" in the hundreds. It runs entirely in your browser and handles values from zero to the trillions.
