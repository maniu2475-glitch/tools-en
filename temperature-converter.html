About this tool

This temperature converter converts between the four most common temperature scales: Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Kelvin (K) and Rankine (°R). Type a value in any field and every other field updates instantly — no clicking a separate convert button. It uses the standard formulas: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, K = °C + 273.15, and °R = (°C + 273.15) × 9/5. Absolute zero is −273.15 °C, and the tool correctly handles negative and decimal values, so it works equally well for science homework, cooking recipes, weather comparisons and engineering data. Everything runs locally in your browser: nothing is uploaded, no account is needed.
