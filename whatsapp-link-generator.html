WhatsApp Link Generator

Build a click-to-chat link

Enter your number in international format without "+", spaces or dashes (example: 14155552671 for a US number).

A WhatsApp click-to-chat link lets anyone start a conversation with you without first saving your number: the visitor taps the link, WhatsApp opens (app or web) and the chat is ready, optionally with a message already typed. The official format is https://wa.me/<phone>, where the phone number is written in full international format with no plus sign, no spaces and no leading zero - for example wa.me/14155552671 for a US number and wa.me/8613800138000 for a Chinese mobile.

Adding ?text= with a URL-encoded message prefills the chat box, which is exactly what you want on a product page ("Hi, I would like to order the blue model"), in a restaurant menu, on a business card landing page, or in an email signature. The generator validates the number length (8 to 15 digits), builds the encoded URL for you and shows both the raw link and an HTML button snippet you can paste straight into a website.

The companion QR code is handy for print: put it on a poster, a menu, a package insert or a shop window and passers-by scan it with their camera to open the conversation. Because the whole flow - validation, URL encoding and QR rendering - happens in your browser, the phone number and message are never uploaded. One caveat worth knowing: wa.me does not guarantee delivery to a user who has blocked unknown contacts, and very long prefilled messages may be truncated by some clients.

Frequently asked questions

What number format does wa.me require?

Country code followed by the subscriber number, digits only: no plus sign, no spaces, dashes, brackets or leading zero. UK 07700 900123 becomes 447700900123.

Why does my link open WhatsApp Web instead of the app?

wa.me detects the platform. On desktop browsers it opens web.whatsapp.com; on phones with the app installed it opens the app directly. Both work with the same link.

Can I use a prefilled message with emoji or Chinese text?

Yes. The message is URL-encoded with encodeURIComponent, so emoji, Chinese characters, line breaks and symbols are preserved.

Is the generated link permanent?

The link is just a URL - it never expires. It stops working only if the phone number stops using WhatsApp or the account is deleted.

Does wa.me work for WhatsApp Business accounts?

Yes, Business accounts can be reached with the same click-to-chat link; customers simply see the business profile banner when the chat opens.
