About the Inches to cm Converter

The inch to centimeter relationship is exact by international agreement: one inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters, defined by the international yard and pound accord of 1959. That means every conversion here is mathematically precise, not an approximation — 32 inches is exactly 81.28 cm, because 32 × 2.54 = 81.28.

Mixed-unit measurements like 5 feet 9 inches are common in daily life but awkward to convert mentally. Using the feet (decimal) field you can enter 5.75 ft for 5′9″ and read the metric equivalent instantly. TV screen sizes, monitor diagonals, waist measurements, paper sizes and furniture dimensions are the most frequent uses.

All four fields stay synchronized as you type, so you can round-trip a value to check it, and full floating-point precision is preserved internally before display. The tool runs entirely in your browser, works offline once loaded, and requires no installation.
