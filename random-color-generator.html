About this tool

Generate random colors with one click and copy their HEX codes instantly. Every click produces a fully random RGB value shown as a large swatch together with its rgb() notation and a note on whether the colour reads as light or dark — useful when picking text colours for contrast. The history strip keeps your recent colours as clickable chips: press one and the HEX code is copied to your clipboard automatically. Designers use random palettes like this to break out of habitual colour choices, spark inspiration for backgrounds, or find a starting hue to tweak in a palette editor. Teachers use it for probability and colour-mixing demonstrations. Because everything is computed locally with your browser's random generator, it works instantly and offline, and no colour history ever leaves your device.
