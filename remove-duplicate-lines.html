About this duplicate line remover

Working with exported lists - email addresses, keywords, log entries, product SKUs or user IDs - often means fighting duplicates. This tool deduplicates any plain-text list line by line, keeping the first occurrence of each line and discarding later repeats. Options let you trim leading/trailing whitespace before comparing (so apple and apple  are recognized as the same) and compare case-insensitively, which is handy for messy CSV exports and mixed-case email lists.

The remove blank lines option also sweeps away empty rows that would otherwise clutter your output. Everything runs client-side in JavaScript, so lists of tens of thousands of lines are processed instantly and nothing is uploaded. Combined with the Text sorter and Case converter in this toolbox, you can clean, sort and normalize lists in seconds without installing any spreadsheet software.
