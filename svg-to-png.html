SVG to PNG Converter

Rasterize SVG vector graphics into crisp PNG images at the exact size you need. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

About this SVG to PNG Converter

SVG is a vector format, which means it scales infinitely without losing quality but is not always supported by the programs or platforms you need it for. Photos, social media banners, app icons, slides and many CMS systems expect a raster format such as PNG. This SVG to PNG converter bridges that gap by rendering your vector artwork into a transparent PNG at whatever resolution you specify. Unlike many converters, the rendering happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API, so proprietary logos or unreleased designs you upload remain on your own machine.

Choose an SVG file, set the output width and height in pixels, and optionally type a background colour (leave the field as transparent to preserve alpha). Clicking Convert to PNG draws the vector onto an HTML canvas at the chosen size and exports a PNG. Because the image is drawn at the requested pixel dimensions rather than being upscaled afterwards, the result stays sharp — a great way to produce a high-resolution icon or favicon from a small vector. A preview of the generated PNG appears so you can check the result, and the file downloads automatically.

For best results, request a size that matches the aspect ratio of your SVG; otherwise the image is stretched to fill the canvas. If your SVG references external fonts, images or stylesheets by URL, the browser may block them for security reasons and the render can fail — inline those assets or convert fonts to paths first. Simple geometric logos, icons and illustrations convert perfectly. You can generate several sizes in a row, for example a 512×512 app icon plus a 32×32 favicon, simply by changing the numbers and converting again. All output is a standard PNG that opens anywhere.
