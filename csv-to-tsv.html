CSV ⇄ TSV Converter

About this tool

CSV (comma-separated values) and TSV (tab-separated values) are the two most common plain-text spreadsheet formats. TSV is preferred by Unix tools, databases like PostgreSQL's COPY, and bioinformatics pipelines because tabs almost never appear in data, so no quoting is needed; CSV is the default everywhere else. Converting between them is tricky when fields contain commas or quotes - a naive find-and-replace will corrupt your data. This converter properly parses quoted fields ("" escapes a quote inside a quoted field), handles multi-line quoted cells, and quotes fields as needed when going back to CSV. All parsing happens in your browser.
