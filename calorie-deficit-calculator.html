Calorie Deficit Calculator

Maintenance calories (Mifflin-St Jeor) plus the daily deficit you need to hit your goal weight - everything runs in your browser.

About this tool

Weight loss ultimately comes down to energy balance: eat fewer calories than you burn and the difference is drawn from stored body fat. This calculator first estimates your maintenance calories (TDEE) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the formula most dietitians consider the most accurate general-purpose estimate for healthy adults, multiplied by an activity factor from sedentary to very active. Then enter your current weight, your goal weight and how fast you want to get there, and it shows the required daily intake and the daily deficit. As a rule of thumb a deficit of about 500 kcal per day produces roughly 0.5 kg (1 lb) of fat loss per week; deficits above 1000 kcal per day are generally discouraged because they cost you muscle and energy. Treat the numbers as an estimate: real metabolism varies, so track your weight trend for two or three weeks and adjust. All math runs locally - your body data is never uploaded.
