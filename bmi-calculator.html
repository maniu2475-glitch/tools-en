About this tool

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a quick screening measure that divides your weight by the square of your height. While it is not a diagnostic tool - athletes with high muscle mass and older adults can be misclassified - it remains the standard first check recommended by the World Health Organization for tracking weight-related health risk. This calculator accepts metric (kg / cm) or imperial (lb / ft+in) units, computes your BMI to one decimal place, places you in the WHO category (underweight, normal, overweight, or one of three obesity classes), and shows the healthy weight range for your height so you know how far you are from it. All arithmetic happens in your browser; nothing is stored or transmitted.
