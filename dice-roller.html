About this tool

A free online dice roller for board games, tabletop RPGs and classroom activities. Choose how many dice to throw, pick common polyhedral sides — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d100 — and add an optional modifier before rolling. Each die uses your browser's random number generator, so results are unpredictable and nothing is sent to a server. A running history panel keeps the last throws visible, which is handy for comparing rounds without writing anything down. The total line already includes the modifier, matching the standard notation like 2d6+3 used in most RPG rulebooks. Whether you lost the physical dice from a board game or you need twenty d20s at once for a horde encounter, this roller handles it instantly and works on phones as well as desktops.
