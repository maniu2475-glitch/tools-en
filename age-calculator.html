Age Calculator

Exact age & next birthday countdown — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

About this tool

Need your exact age for a form, a visa application, a school admission or just curiosity? This calculator takes a date of birth (and an optional "as of" date, which defaults to today) and computes the age in years, months and days the way a person would count it, correctly handling month lengths, leap years and end-of-month edge cases. It also reports the total number of days, weeks and hours lived, plus a countdown to your next birthday - including which weekday it falls on. All date math runs in your browser with the standard JavaScript Date object, so results match what any app would compute, and your birth date is never transmitted anywhere.
