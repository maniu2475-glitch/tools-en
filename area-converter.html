About this tool

Area units trip people up because they convert in squares, not straight lines: one square foot is 0.09290304 square meters (not 0.3048), and an acre is a surprising 4,046.86 m². Real estate listings, land surveys, flooring packages and gardening guides all use different units — square feet in the US, square meters nearly everywhere else, acres and hectares for land, square miles and kilometers for geography. This converter shows all seven common units at once, so you can see in a single glance, for example, that a 2,000 ft² apartment is about 185.8 m², or that a 10-acre plot is just over 4 hectares. Click a unit button, type a value, and every conversion updates instantly as you type. Precision is maintained using exact international definitions of the foot and the acre. All conversion math runs locally in your browser with zero data transfer.
