About this tool

A date difference calculator answers one of the most common everyday questions: how long is it between two dates? Whether you are counting down to a deadline, tracking a project timeline, calculating someone's age, or measuring the length of a contract, this tool gives you the answer instantly and accurately.

The calculator computes several views of the same interval. The total days figure is the raw day count including weekends. The business days option excludes Saturdays and Sundays, which is useful for estimating delivery times, SLA windows, or working schedules. The calendar breakdown shows the interval as years, months and days the way a person would describe it, matching how ages and anniversaries are traditionally expressed.

Everything runs in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. You can also use it in reverse: to find a date that is N days from today, try our related tools below. Dates before 1970 and far into the future are handled correctly, including leap years, because the math uses the browser's native date functions.

FAQ

How are business days counted?

Business days are Monday through Friday. Public holidays are not excluded because they vary by country, so add them manually if needed.

Does it count leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses real calendar rules, so February 29 and leap years are fully accounted for.

What if the end date is before the start date?

The tool swaps them automatically and always returns a positive duration.
