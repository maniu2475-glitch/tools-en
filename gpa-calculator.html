About this tool

Your grade point average is the single number most universities, scholarship committees and employers use to summarize academic performance. It is computed as the weighted average of your grade points, where each course is weighted by its credit hours — a 4-credit A counts twice as much as a 2-credit A.

This calculator uses the standard US 4.0 scale: A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, and so on down to F=0. Enter each course with its credit hours and letter grade, add rows for however many courses you have, and click Calculate. The result shows total credits, your GPA to two decimals, and a plain-language interpretation.

Weighted by credits is the detail most people get wrong when computing GPA by hand: an A in a 1-credit seminar does not offset a C in a 5-credit lab. Letting the tool do the arithmetic avoids that trap. Because everything runs in your browser, your academic record never leaves your device.

Planning ahead? Change a grade to the one you expect to get and recalculate to see how a single course would move your GPA — the fastest way to prioritize studying.

FAQ

What scale does this use?

The standard US 4.0 scale with plus/minus steps (A=4.0 ... F=0).

How are credits used?

Each course's grade points are multiplied by its credit hours, summed, then divided by total credits.

Can I compute cumulative GPA?

Yes — enter all your courses across semesters in one table.
