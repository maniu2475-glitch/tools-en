About the Fraction Calculator

This fraction calculator accepts any two proper fractions, improper fractions or whole numbers (leave the denominator blank or 1). Results are automatically reduced using the greatest common divisor (GCD), so 3/6 becomes 1/2 and 10/4 becomes 5/2.

Working with fractions by hand is error-prone: you must find common denominators for addition and subtraction, remember to flip the divisor when dividing, and then simplify the result. This tool does all three steps for you instantly and shows the decimal value so you can sanity-check the magnitude.

Because everything runs client-side in JavaScript, nothing is uploaded to any server — handy for homework checking, recipe scaling, carpentry measurements and shop math. Bookmark it for quick access whenever you need reliable fraction arithmetic.

FAQ

Does it simplify the result?

Yes — every answer is reduced to lowest terms and shown with its decimal equivalent.

Can I use mixed numbers like 2 1/2?

Convert to an improper fraction first (2 1/2 = 5/2), then enter 5 and 2. The result box also shows the mixed-number form when the fraction is improper.

What about division by zero?

The tool warns you if any denominator or the divisor fraction is zero, since those fractions are undefined.
