About this tool

Planning savings is mostly a compounding problem: every deposit you make earns interest, and that interest earns interest too. This calculator simulates month by month so the numbers reflect how real accounts work. Mode one answers "how long will it take?" — enter your goal, current balance, monthly deposit and the annual rate your savings account or index fund is expected to return, and you get the exact number of months plus a breakdown of deposits versus interest. Mode two flips the question: choose a deadline (for example a house down payment in 5 years) and the calculator tells you the exact monthly deposit required, accounting for interest along the way. Typical rates to experiment with: 0% for cash under the mattress, 4-5% for high-yield savings accounts, and 7-8% for long-run stock market averages. All calculations happen locally in your browser.
