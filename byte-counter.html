About this tool

This counter reports the exact UTF-8 byte size of your text, which is what actually gets sent over the network and what database columns such as VARCHAR(255) or APIs like Twitter/X, Discord, Slack and Google count. A character is not always a byte: a is 1 byte, é is 2, 中 is 3 and 😀 is 4.

Use it to check field limits (e.g. 280 characters vs 280 bytes), to stay under SMS/GSM and JSON payload limits, or to find out why a string that looks short is rejected as too long.

Also shown: character (code point) count, UTF-16 code units, line count, approximate JSON/Base64 expansion and URL-encoded length.
