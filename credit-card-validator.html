About the Credit Card Validator (Luhn Check)

This tool checks whether a card number is structurally valid using the Luhn algorithm (also called the modulus 10 or mod 10 checksum), the standard formula used on virtually every credit and debit card worldwide. As you type, it also detects the card brand from the leading digits: Visa starts with 4, Mastercard with 51-55 or 2221-2720, American Express with 34 or 37, Discover with 6011/65, Diners Club with 300-305 or 36, and JCB with 3528-3589. The length is validated per brand as well (16 digits for most, 15 for Amex, 14 for Diners). Developers frequently use it together with standard Luhn test numbers (for example 4111 1111 1111 1111) while testing payment forms. Important: this tool only verifies the number format and checksum - it cannot tell whether a card is real, active or has funds, and it does not perform any payment. Everything is computed locally in your browser; no number is ever transmitted.
