About this tool

Centimeters and feet are both everyday height units: centimeters dominate metric countries, while feet and inches remain the standard for height in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. One foot is exactly 30.48 centimeters and one inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters, so converting is a simple division once you know the factor. The tricky part is expressing the remainder as inches rather than decimal feet — that is exactly what this converter does for you. Enter a value like 180 and you immediately learn that 180 cm is about 5 ft 10.9 in. The reverse direction is just as easy: type 5 and 11 to see that 5'11" is roughly 180.3 cm. The generated reference table around your value helps you eyeball nearby heights, which is handy when reading size charts, medical records or sports profiles.
