Compound Interest Calculator

Future value with regular deposits — instant, browser-side, no upload.

About this tool

This compound interest calculator projects how a deposit grows over time when interest compounds. Enter a starting principal, an annual interest rate, a term in years, optional monthly contributions and a compounding frequency (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual), and it computes the future value, the total you contributed and the interest earned. It uses the standard compound formula FV = P(1+r/n)^(nt) plus an annuity term for regular deposits, so the numbers match what banks and spreadsheets produce. Use it to compare savings accounts, estimate retirement growth or visualize the power of starting early — small changes in rate or time make a dramatic difference. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
