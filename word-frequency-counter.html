About this tool

The word frequency counter analyses any text and ranks every word by how often it appears, together with its percentage of the total. Writers use it to spot words they unconsciously overuse — filler words like 'very', 'that' or 'really' tend to cluster. SEO specialists paste a competitor's page into it to see which keyword phrases dominate their copy and at what density, since keyword stuffing above roughly 3–5% looks unnatural to both readers and search engines. Language learners use it to extract the high-frequency vocabulary of an article before reading, and teachers use it for vocabulary exercises. The tool lowercases everything, ignores punctuation, and can drop common stop words such as 'the' and 'and' so the interesting words stand out. Results are shown as a ranked table with counts and percentages, limited to the top 100 words for readability. All analysis happens locally in your browser, so private drafts and unpublished manuscripts never leave your machine.
