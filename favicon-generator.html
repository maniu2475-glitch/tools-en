Favicon Generator

Create a favicon from a letter, emoji or image and download all standard PNG sizes — 16x16, 32x32, 180x180, 512x512 — fully in your browser.

All sizes are generated locally in your browser — your image is never uploaded.

About this tool

A favicon is the small square icon a browser shows in the tab, in bookmarks and on mobile home screens. Getting it right matters: a crisp 16x16 or 32x32 icon makes a site look professional, while a stretched or blurry icon makes it look unfinished. This generator produces every size modern browsers and platforms ask for — 16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 180x180 (Apple Touch Icon), 192x192 and 512x512 (Android / PWA manifest) — either from a single letter with a custom background color and corner radius, or from an image you upload. Because everything is drawn in a canvas element on your own device, the source image never leaves the browser; there is no server upload step and no retention of your artwork. Right-click any preview to save the PNG, or use the download buttons. Typical workflow: type your brand initial, pick your brand color, download the 32x32 and 180x180 files, then reference them in your HTML head with rel="icon" and rel="apple-touch-icon". For multi-size .ico files, most modern browsers accept the PNG 32x32 alone, so the generated PNG is enough for 2026-era sites.
